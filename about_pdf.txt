QUALITY 
 
Quality  Built  Into Every  Stage   
At Vesco Science, quality is integrated throughout the entire product lifecycle — from raw 
material selection and process development to manufacturing, testing, documentation, storage 
and distribution. 
Our quality approach is designed to support product consistency, process control, 
traceability and integrity while meeting the defined requirements of each product and market. 
 
OUR QUALITY APPROACH  
01 — Raw Material Control 
Raw materials are carefully evaluated against defined specifications before entering the 
manufacturing process. This helps ensure material consistency, suitability, and quality from the 
very beginning. 
02 — Process Control 
Manufacturing processes are performed under defined and monitored conditions. Controlled 
procedures help maintain consistency, reproducibility, and reliable production across batches. 
03 — Analytical Testing 
Product-specific analytical testing is used to evaluate key quality attributes and characteristics. 
Testing helps verify that products meet their defined specifications and quality requirements. 
04 — Microbiological Testing 
Relevant microbiological assessments are conducted according to product requirements and 
applicable standards. These controls help support product safety, quality, and microbiological 
integrity. 
05 — Batch Traceability 
Each batch is supported by documented manufacturing and quality information. This enables 
traceability from raw materials and production through testing, review, and final release. 
06 — Storage & Distribution 
Products are stored and transported according to their defined storage and handling 
requirements. Appropriate conditions help maintain product integrity and quality throughout the 
supply chain.
